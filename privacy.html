Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

ProjectForge turns a software idea into a structured project blueprint. This policy explains what information the service stores and processes when you use it.

What ProjectForge stores

When you enter a project idea and click Generate Blueprint, the idea is analysed to produce a blueprint (title, description, technology stack, features, tasks, architecture, complexity estimate and estimated build time).

  • Not saved: unsaved blueprints exist only in your browser for the current session and are not transmitted to the server.
  • Saved: when you click Save Project, the idea text and the generated blueprint are stored in a MySQL database, along with the date and time you saved them.

What ProjectForge does not collect

  • No account system, so we do not collect names, email addresses or passwords.
  • No payment information.
  • No location tracking.
  • No cookies, tracking pixels or third-party analytics scripts.

How your data is used

Saved projects are used only to provide the service: they appear in your My Projects section so you can reopen a blueprint later. Project data is not sold, shared or used for marketing.

Managing and deleting your projects

You can delete a saved blueprint at any time using the delete option in My Projects. Saved projects remain stored until you delete them or the data is removed from the database.

Security

Database credentials and configuration values are kept in server-side environment variables and are never exposed to the browser. We do not store your personal contact information anywhere in the service.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Continued use of the service after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the address shown under Contact in the footer of the site.
